The Place

Phalaborwa, Limpopo

Phalaborwa means "better than the south" — and this lowveld town lives up to the name. It sits right against the Kruger Park's fence line, a stone's throw from the bush that made the Great Limpopo famous.

Stay with us and the whole region opens up: game drives through the Big Five country, boat safaris on the Olifants River, open-pit history at the Foskor Mine Museum, and slow evenings on the pool deck while the sun drops behind the baobabs.

  • 2 km — Kruger's Phalaborwa Gate
  • 3 min — town centre, shops & dining
  • ± 45 min — Hoedspruit / Eastgate Airport
  • Self-catering — apartments with kitchenettes
